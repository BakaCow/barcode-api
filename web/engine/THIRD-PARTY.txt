Modifications: lookup rows are represented as JavaScript strings, and the stop
pattern includes its terminating two-module bar. The scanline matching, sample
selection, validation and consensus implementation is project code. This
prototype does not invoke ZXing or another decoder to produce its results.

The prototype supports Code Sets A/B/C, set switching, SHIFT, and GS1 FNC1.
Unsupported FNC2/FNC3/FNC4 and AIM second-position FNC1 are rejected. It requires
approximately ten-module quiet zones and at least two agreeing image rows.
It is an experimental single-value decoder, not a full standards conformance
implementation and not a claim of Scanbot-equivalent performance.
