    How to use it offline

    Open this page with internet once and wait for Offline ready. You can then scan and reopen this same address without internet. Installing it on your home screen makes it easier to find.

    On iPhone or iPad, use Safari’s Share menu → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen option.

    Camera permission and HTTPS are required. Clearing browser data can remove offline access and saved scans. The barcode API’s server endpoints are separate and still require a connection.
